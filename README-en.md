## Features

- Read the Holy Quran comfortably and smoothly with a clean and organized interface.
- Listen to the Holy Quran recited by elite reciters, with advanced features including:
  - Ayahs synchronized with audio during reading
  - Sleep timer
  - Easy and fast navigation between Ayahs
  - Background playback support
  - Reliable translations of Ayahs in multiple global languages
  - Focus mode for a distraction-free listening experience
- Accurately calculate prayer times based on your geographical location, with upcoming prayer display and countdown timer.
- **Tafsir Page:** An advanced search engine providing quick access to Ayahs and words, with trusted tafsir from prominent scholars and imams.
- **Azkar Page:** Includes all remembrances from the "Hisn al-Muslim" book, organized neatly and flexibly for easy tracking and copying.
- Daily reminder card for morning and evening azkar on the home screen.
- Comprehensive settings allowing you to customize the app and features to your personal preferences.
